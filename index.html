I kept every receipt and never looked at one again. A shoebox, a drawer, a photo roll full of paper I meant to sort. If you have ever wondered where the month went and had nothing but a bank total to go on, you know the feeling.

Imagine every receipt filing itself: the shop, the date, each item and what kind of spending it was, the moment you photograph it. That is why we built Snap Your Receipt AI.

What it does

  • Reads the whole receipt. Point the camera at a paper receipt and the app reads the shop, the date, every line and the totals.
  • Files each line under a category. Fourteen of them: Groceries, Eating out, Shopping, Transport, Bills, Home, Health, Personal care, Clothing, Electronics, Entertainment, Pets, Travel and Other. A single supermarket receipt splits into groceries, household and the rest.
  • Lets you correct anything. The reading can be wrong. Change a shop name, a total, a line or its category before you save, and any time after.
  • Keeps personal and business apart. Say what your receipts are for when you sign up; with both, you choose on each receipt. The ones you claim or invoice are one filter away.
  • Finds it again. Search by shop. Filter by category, by personal or business, and by when: last week, last month, any month of the past year.
  • Shows where the money goes. Spending by category and by month, the shops you spend most at, and the bills that come around again.
  • Answers questions. Sage, the in-app assistant, answers using only the receipts you have filed: "How much did I spend on eating out in August?"
  • Takes digital receipts too. Import a PDF or a photo, or share one to the app from your email or files. No receipt to photograph? Type it in.
  • Lets your data leave with you. Export everything as CSV, for any date range, whenever you like.

What it does not do

  • It does not connect to your bank or card. It reads receipts, so it knows what you bought, not just where.
  • It is in English, for Canada and the United States, on iPhone. Android is on the way.
  • It does not sell data and shows no advertising.

Facts, briefly

Pricing

Your first five scans are free. After that, reading receipts is a subscription, monthly or yearly, with a free trial on the monthly plan, billed through the App Store. Everything you have already filed stays readable and exportable either way.

Your account and your data

Sign in with Apple, Google or an email address. Sign-in gives the app your name and email address to identify your account and nothing else. Receipts are stored privately against your account on Google Cloud. Images are read by an AI model (Google Gemini) under paid API terms that do not allow them to be used to train it. How information is collected, used and deleted is set out in the Privacy Policy; the rules for using the app are in the Terms of Use. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from Settings.

Questions people ask

Does it connect to my bank?

No. There is no bank or card connection and none is needed.

What if the reading is wrong?

Correct it. Anything on a receipt can be changed before you save and any time after.

Can I use it for business expenses?

Yes. Mark receipts as business, filter to them, and export them as CSV for your accountant or your own records.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, as CSV, for any date range, from Settings. That never stops working.
