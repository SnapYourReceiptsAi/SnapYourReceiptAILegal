Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Snap Your Receipt AI ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Level Up ("Level Up"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Snap Your Receipt AI mobile application and related services (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, name (if provided), a hashed password if you register with email, and, if you sign in with Google, the identifier, name and email address Google gives us for your account. We do not request access to any other data in your Google Account.
  • Receipt and spending data: receipt photos and PDF documents you scan, import, or share to the app; shop names and addresses, dates, line items, quantities, prices, taxes, discounts and fees, totals and categories read from them; receipts you type in by hand; and recurring expenses you add.
  • Region: your country and state or province, which you choose in the app or which we infer from your device's region setting or from the shops on your receipts. We do not collect your device's location.
  • Questions to Sage: the questions you ask the in-app assistant, and a count of them so that monthly limits can be applied.
  • Referral data: your referral code, a code you enter, and whether a referral has qualified for a reward.
  • Device and usage data: device type, operating system, app version, time zone, and diagnostic logs needed to operate and secure the Service.
  • Camera, photos and files: when you scan, import or share a receipt, we process the image or document you capture or select. The app asks for camera or photo access only when you use that feature.
  • Subscription data: purchase and entitlement status processed through the Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat (subscription management). We do not receive your payment card details.

2. How we use information

  • Read receipts using optical character recognition (OCR) and AI, and sort their items into categories.
  • Store your receipts and show your spending by category, shop and month, including recurring expenses.
  • Answer the questions you ask Sage, using your own receipts.
  • Let you export your receipts.
  • Authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • Process subscriptions and referral rewards.
  • Recognise shops and products across receipts (see section 4).
  • Improve reliability, prevent fraud, and comply with law.
  • Respond to support requests at receipttrackerexp.app@gmail.com.

3. How we process receipts and questions

When you scan, import or share a receipt, the image or PDF is uploaded to our servers and kept in private cloud storage (Google Cloud Storage). PDFs are converted to page images. We read the receipt using OCR on our servers and AI services provided by Google (Gemini), which receive the receipt image and the text read from it, and return structured data such as the shop, date, line items and totals. The extracted data is stored in our database (PostgreSQL, hosted by Supabase). Receipt images and documents remain stored until you delete the receipt or your account.

When you ask Sage a question, the question and the receipt data needed to answer it are sent to the same AI service to produce the answer. Sage reads only your own receipts.

AI extraction and answers can be wrong. You can correct any receipt before and after it is saved.

4. The shared catalogue of shops and products

To recognise that "LOBLAWS" and "LOBLAWS SUPERMARKET" are the same shop, or that two receipt lines are the same product, we build a catalogue of shop names and product names from the receipts filed by all users. A product is added only after the same item has appeared on several receipts from more than one account. The catalogue holds names, pack sizes and the shop's region; it holds no account identifiers and cannot be traced back to you.

We may use prices from receipts, combined across many users so that no individual can be identified, to show how prices compare between shops in your region. Because catalogue entries identify nobody, they are not removed when you delete a receipt or your account.

5. Third-party service providers

We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf, including:

  • Supabase (database hosting)
  • Google Cloud (application hosting, file storage, and Gemini AI)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Apple and Google (in-app purchases) and Google (sign-in)

Their use of information is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information and the app contains no advertising. We share information with the service providers described above, if required by law, or to protect rights and safety. Your receipts are not visible to other users.

7. Data retention

We retain your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your user record, your receipts and their line items, and the receipt images and documents in storage, subject to backup retention for a limited period and legal obligations. Entries in the shared catalogue described in section 4 remain, because they identify nobody.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encryption in transit (HTTPS), private file storage with time-limited access links, access controls, and authenticated APIs. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Correct or delete any receipt in the app.
  • Export your receipts as a CSV file under Profile → Settings → Export receipts.
  • Change your region under Profile → Settings.
  • Delete your account under Profile → Settings → Delete my account (permanent).
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play settings.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable by law.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International users

If you use the Service from outside Canada, your information may be processed in countries where our providers operate, including the United States.
